How to Get Brass Objects Shiny Again

How to Get Brass Objects Shiny Again Brass items around the house can lose their luster over time, but bringing them back to their original shine is easier than you might think. Method 1: Lemon and Salt (Most Effective) This is my go-to method for heavily tarnished brass: Cut a lemon in half Sprinkle salt on the exposed flesh Rub the brass object with the lemon, applying firm pressure Wipe clean with a soft cloth Rinse with warm water and dry immediately The citric acid in lemon combined with salt’s abrasive properties removes tarnish quickly. ...
